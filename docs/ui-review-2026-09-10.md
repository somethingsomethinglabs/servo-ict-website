# Servo ICT UI review

## Scope

The review covered the landing page from the header and hero through the project cards, process, about section, consultation form, and footer. The goal was a consistent typographic system, readable colour contrast, restrained rounding, and clean mobile reflow.

## Findings and changes

1. **Header and hero: healthy after changes.** The page declared Inter but did not ship it, so the exact typeface depended on the visitor's device. The highlighted half of the headline also switched to Georgia. The site now self-hosts Inter Variable and uses it for every element, including form controls. The colour change in the second headline line remains, but its typeface, weight, style, and tracking now match the first line.
2. **Statement and project cards: healthy after changes.** The statement switched to Georgia while the project headings used Inter. Both now use the same family. Display-heading tracking was standardised at `-0.045em`, matching the original "Websites and web development" treatment. Tiny uppercase labels were raised from 10.9px to 11.5px.
3. **Process and about sections: healthy after changes.** The process heading was the third serif treatment on the page. It now uses the same 700-weight Inter as the other major headings. Body copy keeps a quieter weight and colour, so hierarchy no longer depends on a font-family change.
4. **Consultation form and footer: healthy after changes.** Buttons, inputs, selects, and textareas now inherit the global typeface. Helper text and footer text use `#626277`; this produces contrast ratios of 5.31:1 on the off-white page and 5.94:1 on white. The previous light greys fell below 4.5:1 in several small-text placements.
5. **Shape and responsive system: healthy.** Large panel radii were reduced from 32px to 24px and card radii from 24px to 20px. Pills and circular markers stay fully rounded because their shape communicates their role. At a 427px mobile viewport, all main sections reflowed to one column and the document had no horizontal overflow.

## Evidence limits

The collaborative preview loaded and exposed live computed styles at desktop and mobile sizes, but its screenshot capture failed during this run. The review therefore verified font loading, computed typography, colour values, component dimensions, grid reflow, and horizontal overflow from the rendered page. It does not claim complete visual-regression or WCAG conformance.
